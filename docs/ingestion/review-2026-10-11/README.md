## Evidence and timing decisions

- Three repeated Reddit thread groups are consolidated by thread ID. Both snapshots remain available; no text is overwritten or silently removed. Other distinct pages remain separate evidence attached to a company.
- 46 named customer candidates have direct or explicit index product linkage. Aggreko, Crown Commercial Service and Brown & Brown require corroboration; Volaris Group is a parent-context candidate. Explicit index evidence is weaker than a detailed case study and is labeled separately.
- Three jobs are closed: Advantage Solutions, World Resources Institute and PEAK6. Three show relative recent posting/reposting dates. Six other customer listings were observed open but undated, including Hays; that observation does not establish they remain open today.
- Hays explicitly describes an active implementation, but its undated listing still needs current-status verification. Other job responsibilities are not evidence of dated active projects or open procurement.
- Miami University states October 2026 Student deployment, with publication unknown. Confirm whether it has already gone live. Its 20,000 figure is students, not workforce. Tyson Foods deployment completed September 2024.
- Source windows differ: Reddit prefers 90 days, jobs and partners use the dates recorded in their briefs. January–March 2026 partner publications fit the partner one-year window but should not be described as recent within 90 days. Event dates remain separate from publication dates.
- Existing testing/automation solutions demonstrate usage and prior spending, not an unmet need. No qualified lead, open procurement, budget, economic buyer or verified reachability is asserted.
- Anonymous Reddit discussions stay in a research queue. Provider/product mentions such as ADP, Oracle and Chase do not identify an end-customer employer. Anonymous partner examples and related-story links are not merged into named clients.
- Source-named people and roles are preserved, with review references for follow-up; none are promoted to verified current contacts.
