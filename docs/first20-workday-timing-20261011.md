# First 20 companies: Workday timing follow-up

Research checked October 11, 2026. Same frozen cohort as the first-20 enrichment.

## Findings and date handling

Two previously confirmed exact core dates remain: Iowa State (July 1, 2019) and RMIT (June 7, 2021). No additional exact initial/core go-live day was verified. Four other companies have explicit month-level launches: Banner, Sun Life, Roswell Park and Hilti. Regional, module, year-only, planned and upper-bound signals are preserved below with their scope.

Do not turn a month/year into the first day, copy announcement/publication dates into go-live, or count the start of an implementation project as completion. Student, Learning and VNDLY expansions do not replace the original HCM date.

TechnologyChecker was reviewed as a discovery aid. Its public page describes domain detection; it did not establish a verified exact go-live for this cohort. Secondary database claims are labelled as leads, not silently promoted to confirmed dates.

### 5. Iowa State University

- **Additional evidence:** University report confirms the third Student rollout occurred in March 2024; remaining phases were scheduled through February 2025. [Source](https://www.it.iastate.edu/annual-report-2024-archive).
- **Additional evidence:** October 2023 university notice confirms admissions rollout had occurred June–September 2023. [Source](https://archive.las.iastate.edu/2023/10/10/workday-student-resources-for-you/).
