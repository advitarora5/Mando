## Coverage and date policy

All 23 dossiers contain company facts, Workday modules, deployment findings, contact research, category-relevant evidence, a suggested why-now and explicit gaps. The prepared import includes 48 contacts (42 new) and 23 generated research notes. No scores, category point allocations, tiers or snapshots are written. Existing manual overrides are preserved.

No additional exact go-live day was verified. Explicit month-level launches include Asda (March 2024), Hungry Jack’s (April 2025), Li & Fung (September 2018), Capita (December 2019), and Rochester General (April 2014); Land O’Lakes Time Tracking is a separate May 2023 module launch. Partial dates and project intervals are recorded in notes rather than converted to fabricated first-of-month dates. SNG’s April 28, 2026 announcement date is removed from the exact launch field because it is not a verified go-live day.

Employee counts preserve reported scope, as-of dates, estimates and lower bounds. A public LinkedIn profile does not prove a one-degree mutual connection, buying mandate or willingness to act. Adjacent-tool adoption is distinguished from a committed new purchasing timeline. One publicly published direct professional email is marked unverified; shared support channels are not assigned to buyers.
